Privacy & analytics

Last updated: 22 September 2026.

You can explore our paper projects, follow shop links and contact us whether you accept or reject optional analytics.

Optional Google Analytics

Google Analytics 4 loads only after you select “Accept analytics”. It helps us understand visits and use of the website. Google may process cookie identifiers, pages visited, device and browser information, approximate location and interactions. Analytics cookies can recognise your browser across visits. Advertising storage, advertising user data and advertising personalisation consent remain off.

You can change your choice through “Analytics settings” at the bottom of any page. Withdrawing consent disables further collection through our Analytics tag and removes accessible first-party Analytics cookies. It does not erase data already collected by Google. Read how Google uses information from sites using its services.

Remembering your choice

Your choice is saved in local storage for up to 180 days. If your browser blocks storage, your choice may not persist between pages. Clearing this site’s browser data removes the saved choice. Closing the banner does not grant consent or change an existing choice.

Contact enquiries

When you submit our contact form, your name, email address and message are sent to Web3Forms to deliver your enquiry. We use them to respond and help with your request. Web3Forms also processes technical request information to operate the form and prevent spam. See the Web3Forms privacy policy. Please do not include passwords or payment card details. The form works without accepting analytics.

Shopping and other websites

Any external shop links take you to the provider named in the link. Purchases and payment details are handled on the shop you visit, under its own privacy policy and settings. Papercraft Helipads does not collect your card details through these links. Free PDF files linked on this website can be downloaded without accepting analytics.

YouTube videos

Some pages include YouTube videos. When an embedded player loads or you play a video, YouTube may receive technical information, including your IP address and browser information, and use its own technologies. These players are separate from our Analytics choice: rejecting analytics does not block them. See Google’s privacy policy.

Website delivery

This website is published using GitHub Pages. It may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and browser details, to deliver and protect the site. This is separate from optional analytics. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Privacy questions

Use our contact page for questions about information you have provided. We keep enquiry information for as long as needed to respond, provide support and meet applicable obligations.
