Questions

About printable helipad models

Are these physical helipads?

No. These projects are digital PDF papercraft kits. You print, cut, fold and glue the model yourself.

Is the helicopter included?

No. Helicopters, drones, vehicles and figures in the photographs are display examples only.

What can I display on a helipad?

They can be used with model helicopters, drones, small aircraft, action figures, diecast vehicles and other miniature displays that suit the platform size.

Do I need a 3D printer?

No. These are printable papercraft models rather than STL files or 3D-printed bases.

What is the access ramp?

The access ramp is a separate papercraft section that is built alongside the raised platform and can be positioned next to the finished helipad.
