The format

Printable papercraft — not a 3D printer file

The project is supplied as a digital printable PDF. You use ordinary paper-model techniques to cut, fold and glue the parts into a three-dimensional miniature helicopter display base. No physical helipad or helicopter is shipped.

Print

Print the full-color PDF pages at home or through a print service.

Cut

Use scissors or a craft knife and ruler for clean edges.

Fold & glue

Shape the platform walls and assemble the separate access ramp.

Display

Add a miniature helicopter, figures or scenery that fits your collection.
